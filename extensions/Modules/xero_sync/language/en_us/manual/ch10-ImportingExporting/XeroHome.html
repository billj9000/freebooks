Xero Sync

Xero Sync copies your sales invoices and credit memos into Xero, the online accounting service, so that Xero holds the same sales as FreeBooks. It is part of the optional Xero Sync module, installed from Company → Configuration → Modules.

Each document arrives in Xero as a draft, with its lines, accounts and tax rates translated into Xero's own. Before anything is sent, tell the module how your FreeBooks accounts, tax rates and customers correspond to Xero's: see Xero Sync Settings.

How totals are kept equal

Xero works out the tax on each line separately and rounds each one, where FreeBooks totals the tax first and rounds once, so the two can differ by a penny. FreeBooks' total is treated as correct: any difference is added to the Xero document as a Rounding Adjustment line, posted to the rounding account you choose. A real line's amount is never changed.

A document-level discount becomes a Discount line. When every taxed product on the document has the same tax rate, the discount line carries that rate too, so the tax is worked out on the discounted total as FreeBooks does. When the products have different rates, the discount line is left untaxed and the document is flagged for you to check.

FreeBooks stores every amount in your home currency. A document in another currency is converted at the document's own exchange rate before it is sent, so Xero receives the amounts the customer was charged.
