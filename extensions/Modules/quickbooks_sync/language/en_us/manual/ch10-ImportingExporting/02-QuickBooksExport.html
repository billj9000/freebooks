Exporting to QuickBooks

The export page writes your sales invoices and credit memos into files that QuickBooks Online's own import reads. Open it from Customers → QuickBooks Sync. You need permission on QuickBooks Sync, set for each user in the usual way.

Choosing the documents

Enter a From and a To date. Every invoice dated in that range, both dates included, is exported, and every credit memo too when Include credit memos is ticked.

Preview

Press Preview to check the documents before you download them. The preview lists each document, newest first, with its FreeBooks total, its VAT, the total QuickBooks will hold and any rounding line. The Status column says why a document needs checking: an account or tax rate with no mapping, a VAT or total difference larger than the rounding tolerance, a missing payment reference, or a reason it cannot go in an import file. Documents to check are highlighted. Fix the mapping in QuickBooks Sync Settings and preview again.

Downloading

QuickBooks imports invoices and credit notes separately, so the page has a button for each: Download invoices and Download credit memos. Each file lists the documents in date order, one row per line.

  • Discounts. QuickBooks' imports take no discount, so a document's discount is taken off its product lines in proportion to their amounts, as FreeBooks takes it off before working out the VAT. Each such line says (after discount) and carries its net price. The VAT and the total are the same as in FreeBooks; QuickBooks shows the sale net of the discount rather than a separate discount.
  • VAT. Each line carries its VAT amount as well as its VAT code, so QuickBooks records the VAT FreeBooks charged.
  • Documents left out. QuickBooks' imports take no negative amounts, so a document whose rounding line takes money off, or with a negative line of its own, is left out of the file and shown in the preview; it can be sent through the connection instead. So is a document whose number is longer than QuickBooks allows.
  • Large ranges. QuickBooks imports at most 100 documents, or 1,000 rows, at a time. When a range holds more, the download is a ZIP of numbered files; import them one after another.

Importing into QuickBooks

  1. In QuickBooks, choose the Settings (gear) icon, then Import data, then Invoices or Credit notes.
  2. Upload the file. QuickBooks matches each column to its own field; check the matches before you go on.
  3. QuickBooks then asks you to match the VAT codes in the file to its own. The file uses the names from the VAT code mapping, so each should match the QuickBooks code of the same name.
  4. Check the documents QuickBooks lists, then import them.

Each customer and each product or service should already exist in QuickBooks under the name the file gives it: the customer's name as the preview shows it, and the items from the settings page. To keep FreeBooks' document numbers, Custom transaction numbers must be switched on in QuickBooks.

Import each file once only, and do not also send the same documents through the connection.
