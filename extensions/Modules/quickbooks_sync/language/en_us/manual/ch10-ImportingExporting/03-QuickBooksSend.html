Sending Documents to QuickBooks

Once FreeBooks is connected to QuickBooks (see QuickBooks Sync Settings), the export page can send invoices and credit memos straight to QuickBooks instead of through import files. FreeBooks then remembers what it has sent, so nothing goes twice, and protects a sent document from changes that would leave QuickBooks' copy out of step.

Sending

  1. Open Customers → QuickBooks Sync, choose the dates and press Preview, as for the import files (see Exporting to QuickBooks).
  2. Every document that can be sent is ticked. Untick any you want to leave out.
  3. Press Send ticked documents to QuickBooks. The documents go one at a time, and each row's In QuickBooks column shows the result as it arrives. A document QuickBooks refuses does not stop the rest.

QuickBooks has no drafts: a document sent is an invoice or credit memo in QuickBooks straight away, owed by or to the customer. It carries FreeBooks' VAT, its discount as QuickBooks' own discount line, and its shipping address. A customer QuickBooks does not have yet, under the name the preview shows, is added to QuickBooks with the billing and shipping addresses, email and currency of the document. QuickBooks fixes a customer's currency when the customer is added, so a later document in another currency is refused for that customer.

A document with an unmapped account or tax rate cannot be ticked: QuickBooks would book it to the wrong place. Add the mapping, preview again, and send it then. Sending needs edit permission on QuickBooks Sync.

The In QuickBooks column

  • Not sent — FreeBooks has not sent the document.
  • Sent, with the date — QuickBooks holds the document. It cannot be ticked again.
  • Changed since it was sent — the document as it would be sent now differs from what QuickBooks holds, usually because the item or VAT code mapping has changed since. To send it again, release it first.
  • Not sent: with QuickBooks' reason — QuickBooks refused the document. Correct the cause and send it again.
  • Released — see below.

Sent documents are locked

An invoice or credit memo that has been sent shows an In QuickBooks badge beside its number. It can still be opened, printed, emailed and used to raise a credit memo, and changes that do not affect QuickBooks or the tax — the shipping method, for instance — save as usual. FreeBooks refuses to save a change to anything QuickBooks holds or the tax rests on: the customer, the billing or shipping address, the billing email, the number, the date, the reference, the currency or any line. A sent document cannot be deleted either.

To correct a sent document, release it, or raise a credit memo instead.

Releasing a document

Press Release beside a sent document to unlock it. This needs full permission on QuickBooks Sync. The document can then be changed in FreeBooks, and sending it again updates the copy QuickBooks already holds rather than making a second one. The update replaces QuickBooks' copy with FreeBooks' version, so any change made to it in QuickBooks is lost.

FreeBooks does not update a document that has a payment or credit applied to it in QuickBooks: QuickBooks would accept the change and leave the payment against different figures. The row shows why instead. QuickBooks also refuses to change a document that has been voided or deleted there, and the row then shows QuickBooks' reason.

A sale recorded wrongly in a paid or voided QuickBooks document is corrected with a credit memo. Where the QuickBooks document can be deleted instead, delete it in QuickBooks, then press Unlink from deleted QuickBooks copy and send again: the next send makes a new document. FreeBooks deletes nothing in QuickBooks. QuickBooks refuses a document number already in use, even by a voided document, so unlinking while the old document is still there leads only to a refused send.
