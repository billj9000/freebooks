# FreeBooks 1.0.0

FreeBooks 1.0.0 is the first public release of FreeBooks, an open-source accounting and ERP system
for small businesses, forked from PhreeBooks 3.7.1. It keeps PhreeBooks' double-entry ledger,
journals, inventory costing and multi-company design, and brings them up to date: it runs on PHP 8
and MySQL 8 or strict-mode MariaDB, every page has been rebuilt in Bootstrap, PhreeForm is replaced
by a new Forms designer, and the code has been through several security reviews. It adds supplier
currencies, payment links, card terminals, partial shipments, product bundles, DHL Express and
Royal Mail shipping, passkey sign-in and optional Zen Cart, Xero and QuickBooks Online modules.
A PhreeBooks 3.7.1 installation upgrades in place on first login.

## Highlights

- **Forms replaces PhreeForm**: a visual canvas designer for invoices, statements, labels and
  receipts, a column-grid report editor with live preview, and existing reports and forms carried
  over (#14, #62).
- **Runs on current software**: PHP 8.2 or later, MySQL 8 and MariaDB in strict SQL mode, utf8mb4
  throughout (#67, #68).
- **New interface**: every page converted to Bootstrap 5 and htmx; jQuery UI and jQuery removed (#26).
- **Security hardened throughout**: SQL injection, XSS and CSRF protection, bcrypt passwords, login
  lockout, safe password reset, no stored card security codes, and passkey sign-in (#19).
- **Getting paid**: send PayPal or Square payment links and invoices from an invoice, and take card
  payments on a Square Terminal (#1, #2, #20).
- **Shipping from the order**: DHL Express and Royal Mail Click & Drop labels, box packing,
  multi-package rate estimates, partial shipments, duty-paid shipments and carrier collections.
- **Ledger accuracy**: fixes to tax rounding, average costing, assembly unbuilds, POS balances and
  vendor discounts, checked by a new end-to-end ledger acceptance suite (#94).
- **Docker image**: a published container image, `ghcr.io/billj9000/freebooks` (#87).

## New features

### Accounting and banking

- Supplier costs are stored in the supplier's own currency and converted on purchase documents (#7).
- Customers and vendors carry a Default Currency, picked up on new purchase lines.
- Customer receipts and payments are settled in the currency of the document they pay, not only the
  home currency (#63).
- Customer Refund and Vendor Refund have their own entries and managers on the Banking menu (#23, #45).
- Starter charts of accounts for the UK and Ireland alongside the US ones; the installer asks which
  country's books the company keeps (#60).
- Each company has its own time zone, used by pages, scheduled jobs, webhooks and the database clock.
- Exchange rates update daily from the European Central Bank, replacing the defunct Oanda and Yahoo
  sources.
- Journal lists offer Last 60 days, Last 90 days and Last 12 months, and remember the chosen filter.
- Freight tax can be chosen per order instead of one fixed rate.
- A module can lock a document it has copied to another system, so posted copies cannot drift.

### Sales and customers

- Credit memos can be raised from an invoice (with Return All), paid out as a refund, and each line
  has a Re-stock box for goods that should not go back into stock (#71).
- Orders and invoices have a Notes field (#41).
- The Sales Invoice Manager shows which invoices have been printed and can print all unprinted ones
  in one batch (#50).
- Customers and vendors have VAT Number and EORI Number fields (#15).
- Postcode lookup fills addresses on contacts, orders and labels (Fetchify or Vehicle Data) (#8).
- A sales invoice with no quantities can no longer be saved (#11).
- Opening a list item and pressing Back or Cancel returns to the list it came from (#16).

### Purchasing and suppliers

- Items record the supplier's own part number (vendor SKU), printed on purchase orders.
- Per-supplier Purchase Order export profiles export a PO in the format a supplier's system expects.
- The SKU Price Importer (optional module) accepts a vendor's short name and carries the vendor's part
  number.

### Inventory

- Product Bundles: sell a group of stock items as one unit without building it; stock is taken from
  the components.
- Master Product Bundles generate one bundle per attribute combination, such as colour (#21).
- Inactive items are hidden from the item list by default, with a checkbox to show them (#70).
- Items have a Manufacturer part number and a Barcode (GTIN/EAN/UPC) field, sent on customs lines.
- Items have a "Ships in its own box" setting and shipping dimensions used by the box packer.
- Item images may be PNG.
- Label printing: user-defined HTML or ZPL label templates with Code 128 barcodes and QR codes, a
  Quick Label Print page and print icons on the item pages (#6).

### Shipping

- New carrier methods: DHL Express and Royal Mail / Parcelforce Click & Drop, with label printing,
  manifests and help topics (#33, #36, #53).
- Shipping Packages page (Tools) to define reusable cartons, with volumetric weight.
- The shipping estimator packs an order's items into those cartons and quotes each service,
  including Royal Mail and Parcelforce prices from imported price lists (#34).
- Packing list showing the contents of each box.
- Partial shipments: a label can send part of an order, and the order stays open to ship the rest
  (#78).
- Duty-paid (DDP) shipments for DHL Express and Royal Mail, and customs data for Northern Ireland
  addresses (#48, #73, #75, #76).
- DHL Paperless Trade: the customs invoice is uploaded electronically (#59).
- Book and cancel DHL Express courier collections from the Shipping Manager.
- Void a Royal Mail label, which also cancels the Click & Drop order (#47).
- Royal Mail labels take delivery instructions (#72).
- Label forms start with the company as sender and can fill addresses from a contact or postcode (#51).
- Tracking numbers link to the carrier's tracking page (#58).
- Labels can be printed in the browser, downloaded or emailed.

### Point of sale and payments

- Square Terminal payment method, including manual card entry and refunds (#1, #27).
- PayPal payment method using PayPal's current REST API: payment links, PayPal invoices and refunds,
  with incoming payments recorded automatically (#2).
- Request payment from an invoice through any method that supports it (PayPal or Square), with the
  status of each request shown on the invoice (#20, #77).
- Receipts printed at the till and labels can go straight to a thermal printer through QZ Tray,
  with the browser print dialog as the fallback (#30).

### Forms, reports and printing

- Forms, a new document and report module, replaces PhreeForm (#14).
- Canvas designer for forms: the real printed page is shown, and elements can be added, moved,
  resized and grouped; all element kinds, including lines and rectangles with a set thickness (#62,
  #65).
- Elements can be shown or hidden per record by a condition, and can show the company's own details.
- Report editor with columns, grouping, sorting, filters, page setup and live preview.
- Create, copy, rename and delete reports and forms.
- Administrators can define datasets (which tables a report may join) without writing SQL, under a
  separate permission.
- Reports can be emailed as well as downloaded or printed.
- The installer and Company Manager ask for A4 or US Letter and set the shipped documents to it (#24).
- Printing a saved document no longer re-saves it (#64).

### Users, security and administration

- Passkey (passwordless) sign-in alongside passwords (#19).
- Password reset by a single-use emailed link.
- Email can be sent through Gmail or Microsoft 365 using OAuth2.
- Company backups stream to the browser, so large companies back up without running out of memory,
  and backups can be restored in full from the Restore page (#42, #52).
- System Information replaces the PHP Info tab and shows module versions.
- The error, SQL-error and blank-value logs rotate at a set size.
- The Roles page shows one row per permission, and permissions that collided are separated (#55, #56,
  #69).
- New Bootstrap navigation bar, login page, dashboard and My Profile page.
- Rewritten Help manual covering the new features, with context Help buttons (#13, #25).

### Integrations

- Read-only REST API for contacts, invoices, credit memos and tax rates, with API key management (#9).
- *Optional modules in `extensions/`:*
  - **Zen Cart**: order import with payments, addresses, currency and shipping method; stock, price
    sheet and attribute sync that sends only what changed; product upload with images and
    descriptions; shipping notifications with tracking links; VAT invoices emailed to VAT-registered
    customers (#31, #35, #37, #39, #44, #54).
  - **Xero Sync**: send invoices and credit memos to Xero, or export them as CSV (#88).
  - **QuickBooks Sync**: send invoices and credit memos to QuickBooks Online with UK VAT, or export
    them as files (#91).
  - **VIN Lookup**: vehicle details from a UK registration number (#17).
  - UPS and YRC shipping methods remain available in `extensions/`.

### Installation and deployment

- Published container image `ghcr.io/billj9000/freebooks` for x86 and ARM, with a Docker Compose
  setup in the freebooks-docker repository (#87).
- The installer checks PHP and its extensions, refuses to run once FreeBooks is configured, and works
  behind HTTPS proxies.
- `.htaccess` rules work on Apache 2.4 without the compatibility module.
- Composer-managed libraries are committed, so there is still no build step.

### Languages

- British English added (#61).
- Dutch is complete; Portuguese (Brazil) and Russian language files are complete enough that every
  page opens, with untranslated phrases in English.

## Major fixes

### Ledger, tax and costing

- Tax is rounded once per tax authority per document; it was posted to fractions of a penny,
  leaving small balances in receivables (#105).
- Average-cost stock added by a credit memo, assembly or adjustment is valued at the item's average,
  not 0 (#113).
- An assembly unbuild returns parts at the cost the journal posts (#103).
- Vendor early-payment discounts post to a discount account, not Accounts Payable, on the US charts
  (#98).
- POS sales, POS purchases and till entries now carry balances forward to later periods and years
  (#108); till cash movements reach the account balances.
- A free item line no longer takes the previous line's price as its cost.
- Purchases received from an order without an AP account no longer post to a blank account, and the
  ledger refuses any posting to an account that is not in the chart.
- Full Price With Tax stays in step with Full Price wherever a price is set.
- Quantity on hand on the item page matches the item list (#93).
- "Fill order with items with low stock" no longer fills the order with nearly every item (#57).

### Currency

- Amounts in a currency that is not set up are shown correctly instead of as 0.
- Reopened payments show the correct currency for fully paid invoices.

### Pages and saves that failed

- On strict database servers, including MySQL 8 at its defaults: General Journal entries, quote
  conversion, POS sales, CAPA records and many other saves now succeed (#80, #95, #97, #110).
- Creating a company by copying an existing one works on a standard MariaDB server (#66); contacts of
  every type can be deleted.
- Names, addresses and descriptions are no longer cut off at 32 characters.
- Fixed: General Journal row deletion, orders whose lines have no SKU (#12), the POS screen for users
  without configuration rights (#111), the Budget page, the Import Bank and Assemblies settings, the
  Assets settings page (#79), the Translator list (#81) and the Shipping settings page (#82).
- PHP 8 errors throughout printing, the installer and the order, payment and item pages.

## Security

FreeBooks has had several full security reviews since the fork. The main changes:

- SQL injection closed across the core, every module and the extensions; list searches are built in
  one place and escape wildcards (#83).
- Cross-site scripting fixes, and CSRF tokens on every form submission.
- Requests can only reach installed modules and their own pages; file paths from requests are checked.
- Passwords stored with bcrypt (old hashes upgraded on login), login lockout after repeated failures,
  and hardened session cookies.
- Lost-password requests send a single-use link instead of a new password.
- Card security codes are no longer stored with receipts or saved cards.
- Payment webhooks are signature-verified (PayPal also supports a shared secret).
- User-written label templates run in a sandbox.
- Configuration, company files, backups, logs and documentation are kept out of reach of the web.
- Passkey sign-in, which requires HTTPS.

## Removed or replaced

- **PhreeForm** removed; replaced by **Forms**, which the upgrade installs.
- **jQuery UI and jQuery** removed; dialogs are Bootstrap modals, date fields are native, tables use
  simple-datatables.
- **CKEditor** replaced by **Jodit**.
- **jzebra** (Java applet) replaced by **QZ Tray**.
- **TCPDF** 5.9 replaced by TCPDF 6.11; **PHPMailer** 5 by PHPMailer 7.
- **PayPal NVP** (Website Payments Pro) card method removed; use the new PayPal method.
- **PaymentTech** method removed; it could not be installed and did not work.
- **BigDump** restore page replaced by a native restore page.
- Oanda and Yahoo exchange-rate sources replaced by the European Central Bank.
- Zen Cart's SOAP connection replaced by a JSON API.
- The old drop-down menu replaced by a Bootstrap navigation bar.
- The PHP Info tab replaced by System Information.

## Upgrading from PhreeBooks 3.7.1

1. Take a backup of the database and `my_files/`.
2. Deploy FreeBooks over the existing installation, keeping `includes/configure.php` and `my_files/`.
3. Log in. The schema is brought up to date on first login to each company, and each module records
   the new version. Every step checks whether it has already been applied, so an interrupted upgrade
   can be re-run.

PhreeForm is replaced by Forms, which the upgrade installs; existing reports and forms carry over.
Anyone who could use PhreeForm can run and write reports in Forms. Defining datasets is a new
permission, granted under Company → Roles.

Older PhreeBooks releases must first be upgraded to 3.7.1 with PhreeBooks' own updater.

## Requirements

- PHP 8.2 or later (8.x), with `pdo_mysql`, `mbstring`, `curl`, `openssl`, `gd`, `zip`, `bz2` and
  `session`.
- MySQL 8.0 or later (tested on 8.4), or MariaDB 10.6 or later (tested on 11.4).
- Apache 2.4 with `mod_rewrite` and `AllowOverride All`. Nginx is untested.
- HTTPS recommended; required for passkey sign-in.
- Or Docker: `ghcr.io/billj9000/freebooks`, with the Compose setup in
  [freebooks-docker](https://github.com/billj9000/freebooks-docker).
