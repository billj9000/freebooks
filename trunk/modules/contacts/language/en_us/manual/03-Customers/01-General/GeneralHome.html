Customers - General Information

This section covers the activites realted to your customers.

Accounts - Adding, editing, and deleting customer accounts.

Sales Orders - Creating and managing sales orders for a customer.

Sales Quotes - Recording a price quote for a customer and converting it to a sales order.

Sales/Invoices - Creating a sale/invoice and managing the Invoice Manager.

Point Of Sale - Ringing up a sale on the dedicated POS register screen.

Credit Memos - Recording a credit memo issued to a customer.

Customer Status - Marking a customer active or inactive.
