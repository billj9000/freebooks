Deleting a Customer Credit Memo

Deleting a credit memo reverses its general ledger entry and, for any line that had Re-stock ticked, reverses the inventory it returned. Deletion requires Full security rights on Customer Credit Memos, and if the "restrict to current period" preference is set, the credit memo can only be deleted while it still falls in the current accounting period.

  1. From the Customers Menu, select Credit Memos. The Credit Memo form will load.
  2. Press the Open button and a popup will appear listing the credit memos for the current period. Select the credit memo to be deleted and the credit memo form will be filled in.

    NOTE: Use the period pull down and search box to facilitate locating the desired credit memo.
  3. Press the Delete button and confirm the action on the confirmation popup.
