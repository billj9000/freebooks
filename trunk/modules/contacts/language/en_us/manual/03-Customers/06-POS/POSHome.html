Customers - Point of Sale

Point of Sale (POS) is a dedicated register screen, not the standard Sales/Invoice window. It is provided by its own module (Point of Sale) with its own menu items and its own journal (J19).

Menu Access

  • Customers → Point of Sale — opens the POS register screen for ringing up a sale.
  • Banking → Point of Sale → POS/POP Manager — search, view, and reprint past POS transactions.
  • Banking → Point of Sale → Closing POS/POP — count and reconcile a till at the end of a shift.
  • Banking → Receipts → Customer Deposits and Banking → Vendor Payments → Vendor Deposits — record a till's cash deposit to the bank.

Ringing Up a Sale

  1. Open Customers → Point of Sale.
  2. Choose the active till from the till drop-down (if more than one till is configured).
  3. Click Select Customer to look up the customer, or leave it on the default account for a walk-in sale.
  4. Add items to the sale the same way as on a sales order.
  5. Click Pay to open the payment popup, enter the tender, and complete the sale. The transaction posts immediately as a Point of Sale (J19) entry — there is no separate invoice-then-receipt step as there is on the standard Sales/Invoice window.

The Open Sales button reopens a held/parked sale, and Other Transactions records non-inventory till activity (such as a paid-out). At the end of a shift, use Closing POS/POP to reconcile the till.
