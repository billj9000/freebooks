Customers - Status

There is no separate status screen for a customer. A customer's active/inactive state is a single Inactive checkbox on the customer's General tab.

When checked, the customer is excluded from the default customer list (a Show Inactive option on the list toggles them back into view) and its row is highlighted when shown alongside active customers. Checking Inactive does not delete any history or prevent existing transactions from referencing the customer — it only affects whether the customer appears in day-to-day lists and lookups.
