Vendors - General Information

This section covers the activites realted to your vendors.

Accounts - Adding, editing, and deleting vendor accounts.

Purchase Orders - Creating and managing purchase orders (RFQs and POs) sent to a vendor.

Quotes - Recording vendor price quotes and converting them to a purchase order.

Purchases - Receiving goods and services against a purchase order.

Payments - Paying a vendor by single or bulk payment.

POP - Point of Purchase: receiving an item and applying a payment at the same time.

Credit Memos - Recording a credit memo issued by a vendor.

Vendor Status - Marking a vendor active or inactive.
