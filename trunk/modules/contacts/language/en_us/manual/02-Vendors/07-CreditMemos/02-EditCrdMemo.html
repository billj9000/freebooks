Editing a Vendor Credit Memo

Editing a credit memo can be performed at any time.

  1. From the Vendors Menu, select Credit Memos. The Credit Memo form will load.
  2. Press the Open button and a popup will appear listing the Credit Memos for the current period. Select the credit memo to be edited and the credit memo form will be filled in.

    NOTE: Use the period pull down and search box to facilitate locating the desired credit memo.
  3. Enter any changes, as needed, to the credit memo. The Re-stock checkbox on each line can be changed — re-saving the credit memo re-posts it and adjusts inventory to match whatever the checkboxes say at the time of saving.
  4. Press the Save button and confirm the action on the confirmation popup.

When a credit memo is edited, the journal entry is re-posted in the database and the quantity on hand values are readjusted for any line with Re-stock ticked. This affects the income statement and balance sheet, since a credit memo represents an actual transaction against the vendor's account.
