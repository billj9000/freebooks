Using PhreeHelp

Many pages in FreeBooks have a Help icon on their toolbar. Clicking it opens PhreeHelp in a separate window, scoped to a topic relevant to the page you were on.

The PhreeHelp window is split into two parts: a sidebar on the left with Contents, Index, and Search tabs for finding topics, and a content pane on the right showing the topic itself. See Search Techniques for details on locating a topic, and Help Buttons for what the toolbar buttons at the top of the window do.
