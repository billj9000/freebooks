Banking - Single Payments

This procedure covers payments via check, eft, or other non-paper method to a single vendor. This method is best used when payment is desired to a single vendor as it allows for easier selection of the vendor of interest. Payments Ref Numbers cannot be duplicated in the system.

To pay a bill to a single vendor - writing a check

  1. From the menu click on Banking -> Vendors -> Vendor Payment.
  2. Enter part of the vendor name to search for and press the open folder icon next to the vendor id field.
  3. A popup will be displayed showing the vendors that match the entered search criteria. NOTE: Only the vendors that have an outstanding bill to be paid will be displayed. If the vendor is not listed, make sure there is an open invoice entered into FreeBooks to pay.
  4. Click anywhere on the vendor name to fill in the Payments form and close the popup.
  5. All outstanding invoices from this vendor will be displayed, including any open vendor credit memos. Click on the Pay checkbox next to all the invoices you wish to pay. If the vendor payment terms have been set, any early payment discounts will be calculated. They may also be entered manually (note that the balance is calculated automatically if a discount is entered).
  6. Enter the Payment Ref #, this is usually the check number to be printed.
  7. Enter the check date, Reference, and account information as necessary.
  8. Press the Print icon to generate the form. The forms of type banking:checks will be displayed. Check the radio button of the form to print, select the delivery method and press the PDF icon.
  9. Print to the pre-printed checks on the printer.

The Payment Ref Num will be automatically incremented.

To pay a bill via EFT, credit card, and other non-check methods

  1. Follow the procedure above, except enter an unique Payment Ref that identifies the payment. Select the correct account and post the payment by clicking the Save icon. To pay by credit card, select a short term liability account to post the payment to.

NOTE: After paying with this method, the Payment Ref Num will auto-increment, probably to something non-desirable. It will need to be re-entered (back to the next check num) when the next run of bills are paid by checks to the next check number.

Documents in another currency

An invoice or a credit memo raised in a currency other than your own is listed here in its own currency, with the code in the Currency column, and the amounts you enter are in that currency too. A credit memo for USD 100.00 is offered as 100.00, not as whatever that came to in your own money on the day it was raised.

What is stored is different, and it is worth knowing why the two are not the same. Every figure in the ledger is held in your own currency, so the entry records the equivalent along with the currency code and the exchange rate it settled at. That is what lets a report add a dollar invoice to a euro one and get a sensible total.

One entry settles one currency. Ticking a document sets the currency for the whole entry, and ticking a second one in a different currency is refused — the entry carries a single exchange rate, and there is nowhere to record a second. Settle each currency on an entry of its own.

The rate used is the one the document was raised at, not today's, so settling a document in full closes it exactly. That is deliberate: it means no odd few pence are left outstanding on something nobody owes any more. If the rate has moved and you want to record the difference, that is a currency gain or loss and this page does not post one for you.

Some payment methods can only send money in your own currency, because they name it when they contact the card gateway. If you choose one of those on an entry in another currency, the entry is refused with a message naming the method rather than quietly charging the wrong money. Record it by another method, or without a gateway.
