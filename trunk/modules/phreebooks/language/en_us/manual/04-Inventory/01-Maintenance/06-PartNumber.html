Inventory - Manufacturer Part Number and Barcode

Customs declarations into the EU ask, for each item shipped, for three identifiers: your own SKU, the manufacturer's part number, and a standard barcode number (GTIN, EAN or UPC) where the item has one. FreeBooks keeps the last two on the item, and shipping modules that print customs declarations send them with each line.

  • Manufacturer part no. — the number the manufacturer, or the supplier you buy the item from, gives the part, up to 35 characters. For an item you make yourself, enter your own code for it. When it is blank, no part number is sent; your SKU never takes its place.
  • Barcode (GTIN/EAN/UPC) — the item's barcode number, up to 14 digits (a GTIN-14 fits). It is sent only when there is one.
  1. From the main menu, select Inventory->Inventory Manager.
  2. Locate the item to edit and click on the edit icon to advance to the data entry screen.
  3. On the Shipping tab, in the Customs section beside the HS code, enter the Manufacturer part no.
  4. If the item has a barcode number, enter it in Barcode (GTIN/EAN/UPC) on the System tab.
  5. Press Save.

Both fields appear for items that are physical products; service, labour, charge, activity and description items do not have them. Copying an item does not copy them, since the copy is a different product.

Where a shipping module's label form lists the shipment's contents, they can also be checked and changed while printing a label, and saved back to the items. How each carrier receives them is described in that shipping module's own topic.
