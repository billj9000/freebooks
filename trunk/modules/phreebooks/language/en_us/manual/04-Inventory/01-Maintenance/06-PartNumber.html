Inventory - Manufacturer Part Number and Barcode

Customs declarations into the EU ask, for each item shipped, for three identifiers: your own SKU, the manufacturer's part number, and a standard barcode number (GTIN, EAN or UPC) where the item has one. FreeBooks keeps the last two on the item and sends all three on every international DHL Express and Royal Mail label.

  • Manufacturer part no. — the manufacturer's own number for the part, up to 35 characters. When it is blank, the SKU is sent in its place.
  • Barcode (GTIN/EAN/UPC) — the item's barcode number, up to 14 digits (a GTIN-14 fits). It is sent only when there is one.
  1. From the main menu, select Inventory->Inventory Manager.
  2. Locate the item to edit and click on the edit icon to advance to the data entry screen.
  3. On the Shipping tab, in the Customs section beside the HS code, enter the Manufacturer part no.
  4. If the item has a barcode number, enter it in Barcode (GTIN/EAN/UPC) on the System tab.
  5. Press Save.

Both fields appear for items that are physical products; service, labour, charge, activity and description items do not have them. Copying an item does not copy them, since the copy is a different product.

They can also be checked and changed while printing a label: the contents table of the DHL Express and Royal Mail label forms shows them for international shipments, and Save descriptions to inventory writes any changes back to the items.

What each carrier receives: DHL Express gets the SKU, the part number and the barcode as separate references on each customs line; Royal Mail gets one reference per line, the barcode where there is one, otherwise the part number.
