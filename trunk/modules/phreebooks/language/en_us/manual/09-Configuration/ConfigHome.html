Configuration

Company-wide FreeBooks settings are found under Company > Configuration > FreeBooks, organized into tabs:

  • General — company name and address, fiscal period settings, and other core preferences.
  • Customers / Vendors — default terms, accounts, and behavior for new customer/vendor records.
  • Chart of Accounts — default GL accounts used by FreeBooks' automated postings.
  • Tax Authorities / Tax Rates (and their vendor-side equivalents) — sales and purchase tax setup.
  • Statistics — record counts and database size for this company.

Separately, Admin Tools (under the General Ledger menu) provides maintenance utilities such as entering beginning balances and database repair/repost operations — it is not part of this Configuration screen.
