Setup Home

Company setup modules allow for customization of FreeBooks to meet the needs of your business. Several modules are included with the standard release of FreeBooks. The standard modules include:

Languages

Currencies

Sales Tax

Countries

States/Provinces

Branches (reached from the Company menu, not Setup, once multi-branch support is enabled)

Shipping

Payment

Price Sheets

ZenCart Module (optional extension — only present if installed)

User Maintenance (reached from the Company menu, not Setup)
