Setup - Languages

FreeBooks is designed for multi-language capability. The standard release includes:

  • English (US).
  • English (UK) — British spelling (colour, cheque, organise) and UK form labels (County, Postcode, National Insurance Number). Menu and page names are the same as in English (US), so this manual applies to both.
  • Nederlands (NL) — Dutch, throughout.
  • Portugues (BR) and Русский (RU) — Brazilian Portuguese and Russian for the main parts of FreeBooks; parts without a translation show in English.

Carrier service names and product names stay in English in every language, and this manual is in English only. All installed languages appear in a drop-down at login. Once a language is selected, it is stored in a session variable and remains in effect until the user logs out.
