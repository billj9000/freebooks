Setup - Company Branches

If your company has more than one physical location — stores, warehouses, or offices that need to be tracked separately — enable multi-branch support to turn on branch tracking throughout FreeBooks.

Once enabled, a Branches menu appears under Company, where each branch is maintained as its own record (name, address, and other details). Branches can then be:

  • Assigned to a user as their default branch, restricting what that user sees to their own branch's data.
  • Selected when transferring inventory between locations.
  • Used to scope reports and dashboards, such as open invoices by branch, to a single location.

Companies with a single location can leave multi-branch support disabled; the Branches menu is hidden and everything defaults to the main company record.
