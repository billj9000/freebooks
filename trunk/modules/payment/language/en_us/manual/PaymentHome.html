Payment Methods

A payment method is how a customer pays you: cash, a cheque, a card through a payment gateway, PayPal. The method is chosen on the Customer Receipt and Customer Refund pages under Banking, and on the Point of Sale screen.

Some methods only record what happened, such as cash or a money order. Others contact a payment service: they can take the payment, check that it went through, or send a refund back to where the money came from.

Installing a payment method

  1. Go to Company → Configuration → Payment Module.
  2. Find the method in the list and click Install.
  3. Click the method's settings icon to show its settings, fill them in, and click Save in the toolbar.

An installed method is shown in green. Remove takes the method and its settings away; entries already recorded with it are not changed.

Currencies

A method that contacts a payment service has to name the currency it charges or refunds in. Methods that have not been set up for this are refused on an entry in a currency other than your own, rather than charging the wrong money. See Banking - Customer Receipts.
