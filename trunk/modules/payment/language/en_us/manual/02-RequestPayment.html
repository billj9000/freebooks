Requesting a Payment

You can ask a customer to pay an invoice through a payment method that supports it — a PayPal or Square payment link, for example, or an invoice PayPal or Square emails to the customer. When the customer pays, the payment method tells FreeBooks and the receipt is recorded for you. Nothing you do when asking for payment records any money, so there is nothing to save and nothing to undo.

Where to find it

  • On a posted customer invoice: the Request payment button on the toolbar.
  • In the Invoice Manager: the Request payment icon on the row of any invoice that is not yet paid, next to the Payment icon.

It needs the Customer Receipts permission at a level that can enter a receipt. The choices offered are those of the installed payment methods that can ask for payment — see Payment Methods. For an invoice in another currency, only the methods that can take that currency are offered.

Asking for payment

  1. Open Request payment. The dialog shows the customer and the invoice's Balance due, in the invoice's own currency.
  2. Check the Amount to request. It starts at the balance due; lower it to ask for part of the invoice. It cannot be more than the balance due.
  3. Check the Customer email, which comes from the invoice.
  4. Choose How to ask, and click Create request.

What happens next depends on the choice:

  • A payment link is shown in the dialog. Copy it to send it yourself, or click Email to customer to email it from FreeBooks, with a copy of the invoice attached. A PayPal payment link can be paid for three hours; a Square payment link does not expire.
  • A PayPal invoice or Square invoice is emailed to the customer by PayPal or Square straight away.

An invoice sent by PayPal or Square lists the invoice's items when you ask for the whole invoice, and a single line — Part payment for Invoice … — when you ask for less.

Close the dialog when you are done. The receipt is recorded automatically when the customer pays: see PayPal and Square for what it records.

Seeing where a request stands

The dialog lists every request made for the invoice, newest first, with its status:

  • Requested — sent, and not paid yet;
  • Paid — the customer paid, and the receipt was recorded;
  • Expired — a payment link that was not paid in time. Make a new request if the customer still needs to pay;
  • Failed — the payment method could not complete the request, for example a PayPal invoice PayPal did not send;
  • Cancelled — withdrawn because the invoice was paid in full another way, so that the customer cannot pay it twice. Square withdraws its links and invoices like this; a PayPal request is left to expire or be ignored.

The latest request also shows as a badge beside the invoice number in the Invoice Manager and under the toolbar of the invoice itself. An invoice can have several requests — a new link after one expires, or one request for each part of a payment.

Recording the payment yourself

You do not need to. If you open a Customer Receipt for the customer, an invoice whose payment has been requested is marked with a note saying so. If you record a payment for it anyway — say the customer paid another way — the automatic receipt never takes more than the invoice still owes, and tells you if the payment method received more than that.
