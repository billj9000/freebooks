Payment Methods - Square

The Square payment method takes card payments through your Square account. You can:

  • take a card payment on a Square Terminal while you enter a Customer Receipt;
  • ask the customer to pay by a Square payment link or a Square invoice, from Request payment on the invoice — see Requesting a Payment.

When the customer pays a link or a Square invoice, Square tells FreeBooks and the receipt is recorded automatically. A Customer Refund for an invoice paid through Square is sent back to Square against the original payment.

What you need

  1. A Square account, and an application in the Square Developer Dashboard. Its access token is what FreeBooks uses. Square keeps separate tokens for its Sandbox (test) environment and for live payments.
  2. The location the payments are taken at. Square takes payments only in the currency of the location's country, so that currency must be this company's own. An invoice in another currency cannot be paid through Square.
  3. For a terminal, a Square Terminal paired with FreeBooks.
  4. For automatic receipts, FreeBooks must be reachable from the internet over HTTPS, so that Square can call it.

Installing and settings

Install the method from Company → Configuration → Payment Module (see Payment Methods), then open its settings:

  • Access token — from your Square application.
  • Location ID — the Square location that takes the payments.
  • Sandbox — Test uses Square's Sandbox, where no real money moves; Production takes real payments. The access token and location must belong to the same environment.
  • Device ID — the terminal. Pair New Terminal shows a code to enter on the terminal; Browse Devices lists the ones already paired with the account.
  • Auto send, Default payment type and Auto print — how the terminal behaves on a Customer Receipt; see below.
  • Email address to notify — an address to tell when a Square payment is recorded automatically, or when one needs a person to look at it. Leave it blank for no email.
  • Webhook signature key and notification URL — from Square, once you have registered the webhook below. Automatic receipts need both.

When you save, FreeBooks asks Square which currency the location takes, and warns you if it is not this company's.

Automatic receipts: registering the webhook

In the Square Developer Dashboard, open your application's Webhooks and add a subscription with this URL, using your own host name. NAME is the company's folder name under my_files/ on the server, which is also its database name — not the title shown on the login screen.

https://your-freebooks-host/square_webhook.php?company=NAME

If this installation has only one company, ?company=NAME may be left out. With more than one it is required, and a call without it is refused, so that a payment can never be recorded in the wrong company's books.

Subscribe it to the event payment.updated. Other events are accepted and ignored. Square's Send test event names a payment that does not exist, so it is accepted and records nothing; a successful delivery there shows the URL and signature key are right.

Square then shows the subscription's Signature key. Enter it in the method's settings, together with the notification URL exactly as you registered it, and save. Square signs every notification with the key and the URL, and FreeBooks refuses any notification whose signature does not match, in Test and Production alike — so a URL that differs by a single character stops every receipt.

FreeBooks also checks each payment with Square itself before recording it, and acts only on payment links and Square invoices it created. Terminal sales, and payments taken on the same Square account for anything else, are left alone.

Taking a payment on the terminal

On a Customer Receipt, choose Square as the payment method and click Send to Terminal. The amount appears on the terminal for the customer to pay, and FreeBooks waits for it. The receipt can only be saved once Square confirms the payment, and it keeps Square's receipt number and payment ID.

  • Auto send — when you arrive from an invoice's Payment button, send the amount straight away (On), after a five-second countdown you can cancel (Countdown), or only when you click (Off).
  • Default payment type — card present (tap, swipe or insert), or keying in the card number on the terminal. It can be changed for each payment.
  • Auto print — open the invoice as a PDF once the payment is confirmed.

Asking a customer to pay

Use Request payment on the invoice, or its icon in the Invoice Manager, and choose Square payment link or Square invoice; see Requesting a Payment. They are offered only for an invoice in this company's own currency.

  • A payment link does not expire, and can be paid once. Copy it, or email it from FreeBooks with a copy of the invoice attached.
  • A Square invoice is emailed to the customer by Square, due the day it is sent. Square adds the customer to its customer list if they are not already there, matched by email address.

Either one lists the invoice's items when you ask for the whole invoice, and a single line — Part payment for Invoice … — when you ask for less.

Once an invoice is paid in full, any other Square link or Square invoice still open for it is withdrawn — the link is deleted and the Square invoice cancelled — so that the customer cannot pay twice. The request shows as Cancelled.

What an automatic receipt records

  • A Customer Receipt dated the day Square reports the payment, against the invoice the request was made for, paid into the account set as the default for customer receipts (Company → Configuration → PhreeBooks Module, Customers tab). Its reference is Square's receipt number.
  • Never more than the invoice still owes. If another receipt has already paid the invoice in whole or in part, only the balance still due is recorded. Whatever Square took beyond it is left out, noted in the log, and, if a notification email is set, reported with the amount paid, the amount recorded and the amount not recorded. If the other receipt was a separate payment, the customer has paid twice and the difference is due back to them.
  • The request is marked Paid, and shows so in the Invoice Manager.

If a receipt cannot be recorded — for example because the payment date falls outside your accounting periods — nothing is kept, and FreeBooks tells Square the notification failed. Square sends it again later, so once the problem is fixed the receipt is recorded without anything further from you. A notification Square repeats is never recorded twice.

Square's processing fees are not recorded: the receipt is for the amount the customer paid.

Refunds

Raise a Customer Credit Memo for the invoice, then a Customer Refund from the credit memo. When the invoice was paid through Square, on the terminal or by a request, the refund is sent back to Square against the original payment.
