Royal Mail Pricing

Click & Drop does not give prices, so FreeBooks prices Royal Mail and Parcelforce services from a price list you import. The Shipping Estimator then shows a price for each service that can carry the shipment. Each company has its own price list and its own country zones.

Open them from Company → Configuration → Shipping Module: click the properties icon on the Royal Mail Click & Drop row, then Price List.

The Price List

A price list is a CSV file with a header row and one row per price band, prices in GBP. Columns are matched by name, so their order does not matter. Each row gives the carrier, service, price channel, format, destination and zone, the band's weights in grams, the price and how VAT applies to it, and the date the prices take effect.

  • Price channel is how the postage is bought: contract (prices negotiated with the carrier), account (business account prices), online (Click & Drop prices), mailmark_franking or stamp.
  • Format is Letter, Large Letter, Small Parcel, Medium Parcel, Parcel, Printed Papers or Any.
  • A band covers weights above its from-weight up to and including its to-weight, so 0–100 g and 100–250 g do not overlap.
  • VAT treatment is one of exempt, plus_vat_20 (the price is before VAT), inc_vat_20 (the price includes VAT) or zero_rated.
  • A row can be marked needs_confirmation where the price rests on an assumption; the estimator then notes price to confirm beside it.

Because a price list can hold your contract prices, FreeBooks does not supply one.

Importing a Price List

  1. On the Price List page, choose the file under Import a Rate File and click Check File.
  2. FreeBooks checks the file and saves nothing yet. If there are problems they are listed by line number; correct the file, click Discard and choose it again.
  3. A file that passes is summarised: how many bands it holds for each carrier, service and price channel, and how many are marked to confirm. Click Import to store it, or Discard.

The checks are: the required columns are there; the file has one effective date; each band's from-weight is below its to-weight; bands for the same service, channel, format, destination and cover do not overlap; every price is above 0; channels, rate types and VAT treatments are ones FreeBooks knows; and, where the file has ex-VAT and inc-VAT columns, they agree with the price.

Price Changes

Price lists are kept by effective date. Royal Mail changes its prices several times a year, so import each new list when it is published, with its own effective date. The Stored Price Lists table shows the list In use (the newest whose date has arrived), any that are Not yet in effect, and older ones as Superseded. A list imported in advance takes over on its effective date by itself.

Importing a file for a date already stored replaces that date's list, which is how a mistake is corrected. A list can be deleted with its Delete button.

Country Zones

Prices are set by zone rather than by country, so each country has a Royal Mail zone (UK, Europe Zones 1–3, World Zones 1–3) and a Parcelforce zone (UK, Zones 4–13). FreeBooks supplies these from Royal Mail's and Parcelforce's published zone lists, and a company starts with them.

  • The Country Zones section lists the countries the published lists do not settle, with a note on each, so they can be checked on royalmail.com and parcelforce.com.
  • To change a zone, click Download Zones, correct the file in a spreadsheet and import it with Import Zones. Importing replaces the whole list; a country code or zone name FreeBooks does not know is refused.
  • Restore Supplied Zones puts back the list FreeBooks supplies.

Settings

Four settings in the Royal Mail properties affect prices:

  • Handling charge — added to each quote, in the company's currency.
  • Royal Mail surcharge (%) — for example the fuel and energy surcharge.
  • Royal Mail international surcharge (%) — added to the Royal Mail surcharge on international services.
  • Parcelforce surcharge (%) — the Parcelforce fuel and energy surcharge.

Surcharges apply to contract and account prices only; online prices already include them. Royal Mail can change a surcharge at short notice, and adds a peak surcharge for part of the year: add the peak percentage to the surcharge while it applies, and take it off afterwards.

How a Price Is Worked Out

  1. Parcels. When the estimator is opened from an order, the items are packed into the cartons set up in Packages for Royal Mail or for any carrier. Otherwise the weight and size entered on the estimator make one parcel. Each parcel is priced on its own, and the shipment's price is their total.
  2. Format. A parcel goes as the smallest format its size fits that has a price for its weight. A light parcel too big for a Small Parcel is priced as a Medium Parcel. A parcel whose size is not known is priced as a parcel, never as a letter.
  3. Destination. A price for the destination country itself is used before the price for its zone. Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man are UK for Royal Mail; for Parcelforce the Channel Islands are Zone 4.
  4. Band. The band that holds the parcel's weight is used. Where several bands fit — such as Special Delivery's levels of cover — the cheapest is taken.
  5. Channel. Each Click & Drop service is priced from your contract prices where the price list has them, then account prices, then online prices. Royal Mail Tracked 24 and Tracked 48 always use online prices.
  6. Not available. A service with no price for one of the parcels — too heavy, too big, or not offered to that country — is not shown. A price is never estimated beyond the price list.

The estimator shows three figures for each service:

  • Cost — the price before VAT, with the surcharges.
  • Freight Quote — the cost plus the handling charge. This is the amount put on the order when the service is chosen.
  • Book Price — the cost with VAT, as charged by the carrier.

The Notes column gives the format and price channel used, the surcharge added, and price to confirm where the price list marks the price. Prices are in GBP; a company keeping its books in another currency is quoted at its exchange rate for GBP, which must be set up under currencies.

When No Prices Appear

  • No price list is in effect — none has been imported, or the only one has an effective date still to come.
  • The destination country has no Royal Mail zone — add the country to the zones file and import it.
  • A single service is missing — the price list has no price for that parcel to that country: check its weight, size and the service's limits.
  • An item has no size in its inventory record — its weight is counted but it is not packed, and the estimator says so. Add its dimensions for an accurate format.
