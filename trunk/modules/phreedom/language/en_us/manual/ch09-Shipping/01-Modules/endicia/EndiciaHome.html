Shipping Modules - US Postal Service (Endicia)

FreeBooks includes a shipping module for USPS rates and labels, powered by Endicia (a USPS-licensed postage provider). The module supports domestic rate quotes, thermal and standard label printing, package tracking, and a postage balance you fund directly from within FreeBooks.

An Endicia account is required. Basic service is normally sufficient (roughly $15.95/month at time of writing — confirm current pricing with Endicia).

Features

  • Domestic rate quotes for the USPS mail classes enabled in configuration (Priority, Express, First Class, Parcel Select, Media Mail, and others)
  • Label generation via SOAP request to the Endicia label service
  • Thermal label printing (EPL2 or ZPLII printer languages)
  • Postage balance purchase ("Buy Postage") directly from Shipping Manager
  • Pass phrase management (required to activate the account and secure label requests)
  • Package tracking by date or by individual shipment
  • Label refund/void requests
  • Shipping Manager integration

Prerequisites

  1. An Endicia account — register at endicia.com. Wait for the confirmation email with your account number.
  2. The temporary pass phrase used during Endicia registration (this is changed to a permanent one during setup, see below).
  3. For thermal labels, a supported Zebra/Eltron-compatible label printer. Older Eltron/Zebra 2442 and 2844 models do not support the barcode types required for USPS postage printing.

Installation

  1. Go to Company → Configuration → Shipping Module.
  2. Locate the US Postal Service row and click Install.
  3. Click the row again and click Edit.
  4. Enter your Endicia Account Number and the temporary Pass Phrase from registration. Save the changes.
  5. Go to Tools → Shipping Manager, open the US Postal Service tab, and click Change Pass Phrase to activate the account with a permanent pass phrase. The new pass phrase is saved automatically.
  6. Back in the module's Edit screen, set Mode to Production for live rates and labels (leave as Test to use Endicia's sandbox environment).
  7. Select the Printer Type (EPL2 or ZPLII) matching your label printer, and enter the Printer Name as configured on the local workstation.
  8. Check the USPS service types to offer, set a Sort Order greater than zero (a sort order of zero hides the module), and click Save.

Buying Postage

In Tools → Shipping Manager, open the US Postal Service tab, select an amount from the postage dropdown, and click Buy Postage to add funds to your Endicia postage balance. Labels cannot be printed once the balance is exhausted.

Getting a Rate Quote

  1. Open a sales invoice or order.
  2. Click the Shipping Estimator button next to the freight method field.
  3. Verify or update the destination address and package details, then click Next.
  4. Rates for the enabled USPS service types are listed. Click the desired rate to populate the carrier, service, and cost on the order; the popup closes automatically.

Printing a Label

  1. Post (save) the invoice first.
  2. In Customers → Invoice Manager, locate the invoice and click the Ship icon on its row, or use Tools → Shipping Manager to ship a package not tied to an invoice.
  3. Enter or verify the package weight and dimensions, then submit the label request.
  4. The label is retrieved from Endicia and stored on the server (thermal labels are saved as raw printer files under my_files). Print it to the configured printer.

Tracking

Package status can be checked by shipment date or by an individual shipment log entry from Tools → Shipping Manager; status updates are reported through the message stack rather than a persistent tracking page.

Voiding a Label

Labels can be refunded/voided from the Shipping Manager log entry. Endicia's own refund eligibility rules apply (labels must typically be refunded within a limited window of being printed).
