Shipping Modules - DHL Express

FreeBooks includes a shipping module for DHL Express using the MyDHL API. The module supports international and domestic rate queries, label generation for multi-package shipments, customs export declarations, and one-off courier collections.

A DHL Express account and API credentials are required. Credentials are obtained from the DHL Developer Portal.

Features

  • Real-time rate quotes from within the order/invoice form
  • Label generation for all DHL Express service levels (Worldwide, 9:00, 10:30, 12:00, Domestic, Economy Select, etc.)
  • Multi-package shipments
  • Automatic customs / export declaration for international shipments
  • One-off courier collections booked and cancelled from Shipping Manager
  • Label reprint from the Shipment Details popup
  • Test (sandbox) and Production modes

Prerequisites

  1. A DHL Express account number.
  2. MyDHL API credentials (API key and API secret) from the DHL Developer Portal. Separate credentials exist for the sandbox (Test) and live (Production) environments.
  3. Company address fields completed in Admin → Company Information (postal code, city, and country are sent to DHL as the ship-from address).

Installation

  1. Go to Company → Configuration → Shipping Module.
  2. Locate the DHL Express row and click Install.
  3. Click the DHL Express row again and click Edit.
  4. Enter your API Key, API Secret, and DHL Account Number.
  5. Set Mode to Test to use the sandbox, or Production for live shipments.
  6. Optionally fill in the five Collections settings: the time parcels are usually ready, the latest time the courier can collect, where the courier collects from (for example Reception), and the contact name and telephone number DHL is given for a collection. A blank contact name uses the receivables contact name on the My Company tab, and a blank telephone number the company's telephone number. They pre-fill the collection form and can be changed there for each booking.
  7. Click Save.

International Compliance Settings (EORI / IOSS)

Shipper EORI and IOSS numbers are configured in Company → Configuration → Shipping Module → General → International Compliance.

  • Shipper EORI Number — sent to DHL on every international shipment as a shipper registration number. Required for exporting from the UK or EU.
  • Shipper IOSS Number — stored for reference only. Because DHL does not currently provide a per-shipment IOSS field in the MyDHL API, the IOSS number must be registered directly on your DHL account at mydhl.express.dhl. Once registered on the account, DHL applies it automatically to eligible EU import shipments (goods valued below €150).

Getting a Rate Quote

  1. Open a sales invoice or order.
  2. Click the Shipping Estimator button next to the freight method field.
  3. Verify or update the destination address and package details, then click Next.
  4. Available DHL Express services and rates are listed. Click the desired rate to populate the carrier, service, and cost on the order; the popup closes automatically.

Printing a Label

  1. Post (save) the invoice first. The invoice must be in posted state before a label can be generated.
  2. In Customers → Invoice Manager, locate the invoice and click the Ship (truck) icon on its row.
  3. A label manager popup opens with recipient address and shipment details pre-filled from the invoice.
  4. Review and adjust the following fields as needed:
    • Ship Date — defaults to today. DHL will advance to the next business day if the request is made after noon UTC.
    • Service Type — select the DHL Express service level.
    • Package Details — enter weight and dimensions for each package. Click the + icon to add packages for multi-piece shipments.
    • Customs / Export Information — shown automatically for international destinations. Complete the Goods Description, Declared Value, HS/Commodity Code, Country of Origin, and Reason for Export.
    • Shipment Contents — the invoice's items, with the quantity going in this parcel. Change a quantity, or click Remove to leave an item out; see Shipping Part of an Order below.
    • Delivery Terms — international shipments only. Duty unpaid (DAP), the default, leaves the recipient to pay any import duty and taxes. Duty paid (DDP) has DHL bill them to your DHL account instead, so the recipient pays nothing on delivery. Your DHL account must be enabled for Duties & Taxes Paid.
  5. Click Print Label. The label PDF is retrieved from DHL and stored on the server; the label viewer opens automatically.
  6. Print the label from the viewer. Close the popup when done.

Shipping Part of an Order

The Shipment Contents table lists the invoice's items, on domestic and international shipments alike, each with the quantity still to send. Lower a quantity to send part of a line, for example when an item is out of stock, has been credited, or will follow later.

  • Click Remove to leave an item out of this shipment; the line is greyed out and its quantity set to 0. Restore puts back the quantity still to send.
  • A quantity can be anything from 0 to the quantity still to send, which is shown beside it.
  • When a line is sent short, choose what happens to the rest: Rest follows later keeps it outstanding for a later shipment; Rest won't be shipped records it as never to be sent, for example because it has been credited. There is no default: the label is not printed until every short line has a choice.
  • The total weight and the declared value follow the quantities. An item left out is not on the customs declaration, and the shipment's goods description comes from the first item that is sent.
  • At least one item must remain: a shipment with every item removed is refused.
  • On a domestic shipment only the items and quantities are shown; the customs columns appear for international destinations.

Later shipments. While anything is still to send, the invoice keeps its Ship icon in Invoice Manager, beside View Shipment, with a Part shipped badge. The next label lists each item with what is still to send; an item already sent, or recorded as won't be shipped, shows Nothing left to ship. Once everything has been sent or recorded as won't be shipped, only View Shipment remains. Service, labour, charge, activity and description items never count as still to send.

Correcting. View Shipment lists every shipment of the order and what each contained. Deleting a shipment makes its items outstanding again. An item recorded as won't be shipped has a Restore button there, which makes it outstanding again.

A shipment that recorded no contents, such as a label from another carrier, counts as sending the whole invoice.

Leaving an item out of a shipment changes only the label. Credit an item the customer will not receive with a credit memo, as usual.

Reprinting a Label

  1. In Invoice Manager, click the View Shipment (log) icon on the invoice row. The Shipment Details popup opens.
  2. Click Reprint Label in the toolbar. The stored PDF is displayed without contacting DHL again.

Deleting a Shipment

DHL has no way to cancel a label once it is printed. Deleting a DHL shipment removes it from FreeBooks only: the shipment log entry and the stored label file. Do not hand a parcel whose shipment you have deleted to DHL.

  1. In Invoice Manager, click the View Shipment icon on the invoice row. The Shipment Details popup opens.
  2. Click the Delete (bin) icon in the toolbar and confirm.
  3. The shipment's items become outstanding again, so the invoice offers Ship once more and a new label can be printed.

If a collection was booked for the parcel, cancel it in the collection window as well, and book again if other parcels still need collecting; see Booking a Collection.

Booking a Collection

A label does not book a courier. Regular collections are arranged with DHL on the account; for any other day, book a one-off collection once the day's parcels are ready.

  1. Open Shipping Manager and select the DHL Express tab. It lists the day's DHL shipments, with the number of parcels and their weight, and any collections booked for that day.
  2. Click Book collection. The collection window opens for the day shown; change the Collection date to book up to 10 days ahead. The shipments listed are the DHL labels printed for that date.
  3. The window shows the collection address. Collections are always booked at the company address; to change it, edit the address on the My Company tab of the company settings.
  4. Check the Ready from and Latest collection times, where to collect from, the type of premises, and the contact name and telephone number. Up to four short notes to the courier can be added, one per line.
  5. Tick the shipments to collect. A shipment already in a booked collection, or sent from another postcode, is shown unticked with the reason.
  6. A label printed before collections existed has no parcel figures stored with it; enter the number of parcels, the weight of each in kilograms and their size in centimetres, and whether the shipment needs a customs declaration. The figures are kept for next time.
  7. Click Book collection. DHL returns a confirmation number, which is shown in the window and on the DHL Express tab. Any warning DHL gives with the booking is shown too.

To cancel, open the collection window for that day, choose a reason beside the collection and click Cancel collection. FreeBooks sends your user name and the reason to DHL. If DHL has merged the booking with another collection from the same address, the merged collection is cancelled. To change a booking, cancel it and book again.

Bookings and cancellations are recorded in the audit log.
