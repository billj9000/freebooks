Shipping Modules - Royal Mail Click & Drop

FreeBooks includes a shipping module for Royal Mail using the Click & Drop REST API. The module supports domestic and international label generation across all standard Royal Mail tracked and signed service levels, customs export declarations, optional returns labels, an end-of-day manifest, and shipment cancellation.

A Royal Mail Online Business Account (OBA) linked to Click & Drop is required. The API key is obtained from within Click & Drop under Settings → API.

Features

  • Label generation for all supported Royal Mail service levels (domestic and international)
  • Package format selection (Letter, Large Letter, Small Parcel, Medium Parcel, Large Parcel, Parcel, Documents)
  • Optional returns label printed alongside the outbound label
  • Automatic customs / export declaration for international destinations
  • Line-item customs table with HS code, country of origin, and declared value per SKU
  • Save customs descriptions back to inventory records from the label form
  • Shipment cancellation from the Shipment Details popup
  • End-of-day manifest (Click & Drop collection closure)
  • Tracking links via the Royal Mail website

Note: Royal Mail Click & Drop does not provide a live rates API. The Shipping Estimator lists all available service levels with no price; enter the freight cost manually on the order.

Supported Services

Domestic (UK to UK)

  • RM Tracked 24 (RM24)
  • RM Tracked 48 (RM48)
  • RM Special Delivery 9am (RMSD9)
  • RM Special Delivery 1pm (RMSD1)
  • RM Signed For 1st Class (RM1CL)
  • RM Signed For 2nd Class (RM2CL)

International

  • RM International Business Mail Tracked (RMIT)
  • RM International Business Mail Signed (RMIS)
  • RM International Business Mail Tracked & Signed (RMITS)
  • RM International Business Parcel Standard (RMIE)

The service codes above map to Click & Drop API service codes. If your OBA contract uses different codes, verify them in Click & Drop under Settings → Services and contact support to update the module's service map.

Prerequisites

  1. A Royal Mail Online Business Account (OBA).
  2. A Click & Drop account linked to your OBA.
  3. A Click & Drop API key. Obtain this from Settings → API within your Click & Drop account.
  4. Company address fields completed in Admin → Company Information (name, address, city, postal code, and country are used as the sender / billing address on every label).

Installation

  1. Go to Company → Configuration → Shipping Module.
  2. Locate the Royal Mail Click & Drop row and click Install.
  3. Click the Royal Mail row again and click Edit.
  4. Enter your Click & Drop API Key.
  5. Click Save.

International Compliance Settings (IOSS)

The shipper IOSS number is configured in Company → Configuration → Shipping Module → General → International Compliance.

  • Shipper IOSS Number — included automatically on international labels for shipments to EU destinations where goods are valued below €150. Royal Mail applies the IOSS number to the CN22/CN23 customs form.

Recipient EORI and VAT numbers can be entered per-shipment on the label form. They are pre-filled from the customer contact record if available.

Getting a Rate Quote

  1. Open a sales invoice or order.
  2. Click the Shipping Estimator button next to the freight method field.
  3. Verify or update the destination address, then click Next.
  4. The available Royal Mail service levels are listed with no price. Click the desired service to populate the carrier and service on the order.
  5. Enter the freight cost manually in the freight amount field on the order.

Printing a Label

  1. Post (save) the invoice first. The invoice must be in posted state before a label can be generated.
  2. In Customers → Invoice Manager, locate the invoice and click the Ship (truck) icon on its row.
  3. A label manager popup opens with the recipient address and shipment details pre-filled from the invoice.
  4. Review and adjust the following fields as needed:
    • Despatch Date — defaults to today.
    • Service Type — select the Royal Mail service level.
    • Package Details — enter the weight for each package and select the package format (Letter, Large Letter, Small Parcel, etc.). Click Add to include additional packages in the same shipment.
    • Include Returns Label — tick to request a returns label be included in the same PDF alongside the outbound label.
    • Customs / Export Information — shown automatically for international destinations. Select the declaration category and verify the line-item table showing SKU, description, HS code, country of origin, quantity, weight, and unit value. Edit fields directly in the table as required.
  5. Click Print Label. FreeBooks submits the order to Click & Drop and retrieves the label PDF; the label viewer opens automatically.
  6. Print the label from the viewer. Close the popup when done.

Saving Customs Descriptions to Inventory

On international shipments, the Save descriptions to inventory button in the Shipment Contents table writes the customs description, HS code, and country of origin back to the corresponding inventory records. This saves time when reprinting labels for the same SKUs in future shipments.

Reprinting a Label

  1. In Invoice Manager, click the View Shipment (log) icon on the invoice row. The Shipment Details popup opens.
  2. Click Reprint Label in the toolbar. The stored PDF is displayed without contacting Click & Drop again.

Cancelling a Shipment

  1. In Invoice Manager, click the View Shipment icon on the invoice row. The Shipment Details popup opens.
  2. Click the Delete (bin) icon in the toolbar.
  3. A confirmation dialog warns that the shipment will be cancelled. Confirm to proceed.
  4. FreeBooks sends a DELETE request to Click & Drop, removes the stored label file, and removes the shipment log entry. The invoice row reverts to the Ship state and can be re-shipped if required.

Note: Cancellation is only possible before Royal Mail has manifested the order for collection. If the daily manifest has already been submitted, the order cannot be cancelled via the API.

End-of-Day Manifest

Royal Mail requires a manifest to be submitted at the end of each shipping day to confirm which orders are ready for collection. Failure to manifest means the labels may not be processed for that day's collection.

  1. In Shipping Manager, open the Royal Mail panel for today's date.
  2. Click the RM Manifest button. The manifest popup opens and lists today's shipments.
  3. Click Manifest Now and confirm the prompt.
  4. FreeBooks posts a manifest request to Click & Drop. A confirmation message with the manifest ID is displayed on success.

Run the manifest once at the end of each shipping day, after all labels for that day have been printed. Once manifested, orders cannot be cancelled.
