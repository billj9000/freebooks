Choosing a Theme and Colour Scheme

Each user chooses how FreeBooks looks for them under Company > My Profile. The choice belongs to your user account only: it does not change what anyone else sees, and it changes nothing about your data or how any page works.

Changing the look

  1. Open Company > My Profile.
  2. In Select Theme, choose the theme. FreeBooks ships one, Cool Blue.
  3. In Select Color Scheme, choose the colour scheme (see below).
  4. Press Save. The new look applies from the next page you open.

The colour schemes

  • start - the original FreeBooks look: a glossy blue menu bar, blue table headings and compact Verdana text. This is the default.
  • refresh - a flatter, lighter look: a plain blue menu bar, IBM Plex Sans text at a larger size, light grey table and panel headings, and rounded buttons and fields. Pages, menus and toolbars are laid out exactly as in start; only their appearance differs. The typeface is part of FreeBooks itself, so it works without an internet connection.

If the scheme saved in your profile is no longer installed, FreeBooks shows start.
