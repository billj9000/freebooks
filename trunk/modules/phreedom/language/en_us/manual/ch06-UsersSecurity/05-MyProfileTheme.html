Choosing a Theme

Each user chooses how FreeBooks looks for them under Company > My Profile. The choice belongs to your user account only: it does not change what anyone else sees, and it changes nothing about your data or how any page works.

Changing the look

  1. Open Company > My Profile.
  2. In Select Theme, choose the look you want (see below). A line under the list describes the one chosen.
  3. Press Save. The new look applies from the next page you open.

The themes

  • Cool Blue (classic) - the original FreeBooks look: a glossy blue menu bar, blue table headings and compact Verdana text. This is the default.
  • Ledger Dark - a dark theme: light text on near-black panels with a soft blue accent, which is easier on the eyes in a dim room. The menu stays across the top, toolbar buttons show their names as in Refresh, and the Help window and its pages are dark too. Anything that stands for paper - the Forms designer's page, a printed or previewed document, a label - stays white, so what you see is what will print. Its typeface, Inter, is part of FreeBooks.
  • Refresh - a flatter, lighter look: a plain blue menu bar, IBM Plex Sans text at a larger size, light grey table and panel headings, and rounded buttons and fields. Each toolbar button shows its name beside a small icon (Save, Print, Help and so on) rather than a large icon alone, with the main action - Save, or New where there is nothing to save - in blue. Otherwise pages and menus are laid out exactly as in Cool Blue. The typeface is part of FreeBooks itself, so it works without an internet connection.
  • Workspace (side menu) - the menu moves to a dark bar down the left-hand side, where each heading (Customers, Vendors and so on) opens in place and the page you are on is marked. A line across the top shows where you are, for example Customers › Sales Order › New Sales Order. Toolbar buttons show their names, as in Refresh, and the accent colour is teal. The button at the top left hides the side menu to give a wide page, such as order entry, the full width of the window; your browser remembers the choice. On a narrow screen, such as a phone, the menu is hidden until you press the menu button at the top. Like Refresh, its typeface is part of FreeBooks.

If the theme saved in your profile is no longer installed, FreeBooks shows Cool Blue.
