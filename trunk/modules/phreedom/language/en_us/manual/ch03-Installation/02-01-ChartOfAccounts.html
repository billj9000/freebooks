Chart of Accounts

FreeBooks ships with four example charts of accounts, and loads one of them automatically the first time a company is set up:

  • US - General store (LLC and Partnerships)
  • US - General store (Corporations, S Corporations)
  • US - Retail store (LLC and Partnerships) — the default
  • US - Retail store (Corporations, S Corporations)

NOTE: the installer currently loads the Retail (LLC/Partnerships) chart automatically; there is no setup-time option to pick a different one of the four, and no way to supply a custom chart of your own. If a different starting chart is needed, the closest options today are to edit accounts afterward through General Ledger > Chart of Accounts, or to have a developer point the installer at a different chart file (see the file format below) before running setup.

Each chart is an XML file under modules/phreebooks/language/en_us/charts/. An account entry looks like this:

<account>
  <id>1100</id>
  <description>Accounts Receivable</description>
  <heading>0</heading>
  <primary></primary>
  <type>2</type>
</account>

The Account types (the <type> value) are:

0 - Cash
2 - Accounts Receivable
4 - Inventory
6 - Other Current Assets
8 - Fixed Assets
10 - Accumulated Depreciation
12 - Other Assets
20 - Accounts Payable
22 - Other Current Liabilities
24 - Long Term Liabilities
30 - Income
32 - Cost of Sales
34 - Expenses
40 - Equity - Doesn't Close
42 - Equity - Gets Closed
44 - Equity - Retained Earnings
