Reports and Forms

FreeBooks includes a built-in report designer and print engine (the Forms module) used throughout the application to list data, print documents, and export information. Every printed invoice, packing slip, check, and label, as well as every tabular report, is built and rendered by this same engine.

There are two kinds of documents:

  • Reports — tabular listings of data (rows and columns), such as a sales summary or an aged receivables listing. Reports can be exported as PDF, CSV, XML, or HTML.
  • Forms — documents with a fixed, positioned layout, such as an invoice, a check, or a shipping label. Forms always render as PDF. "Letters" (mail-merge style templated documents) are built as a specialized kind of Form rather than a separate document type.

Where to find it

Reports and forms are listed separately, because what you do with one is not what you do with the other.

  • Tools > Reports lists the reports, grouped under the area each belongs to — Customers, Vendors, General Ledger, Inventory and so on — with a filter box for finding one by name.
  • Tools > Form Layout lists the forms, which are laid out rather than run. A form prints one record at a time, so it is opened from the document it belongs to and not from a list.
  • Tools > Report Datasets defines which tables a report may draw on. It is the rarest of the three to hold.

Those are three separate permissions rather than one: running and printing, writing a report or form, and defining datasets. A role can be given any of them without the others, so somebody may print every invoice in the company and still not be able to change how one looks. There is no permission per individual report.
