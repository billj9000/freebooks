Reports and Forms

FreeBooks includes a built-in report designer and print engine (the Forms module) used throughout the application to list data, print documents, and export information. Every printed invoice, packing slip, check, and label, as well as every tabular report, is built and rendered by this same engine.

There are two kinds of documents:

  • Reports — tabular listings of data (rows and columns), such as a sales summary or an aged receivables listing. Reports can be exported as PDF, CSV, XML, or HTML.
  • Forms — documents with a fixed, positioned layout, such as an invoice, a check, or a shipping label. Forms always render as PDF. "Letters" (mail-merge style templated documents) are built as a specialized kind of Form rather than a separate document type.

Where to find it

The main entry point is Tools > Reports, which lists every report and form you have access to. Most other areas of FreeBooks also have their own Reports menu entry — under Customers, Vendors, Employees, Inventory, Banking, and General Ledger — which opens the same browser pre-filtered to that area's reports and forms.

Access to Reports and Forms is controlled by a single permission level that covers the whole module; there isn't a separate permission per report.
