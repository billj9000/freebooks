Glossary

Assembly
An inventory item built from a bill of other inventory items (components), used for kitting or light manufacturing.
Branch (Store)
One of a company's physical locations, when multi-branch support is enabled. Branches are maintained as contact records and referenced internally as "stores" (e.g. a user's default store); the two terms mean the same thing in FreeBooks.
Chart of Accounts
The full list of General Ledger accounts a company posts transactions to.
Company
A single set of books in FreeBooks. Each company has its own isolated database, so data never mixes between companies on the same installation.
Credit Memo
A transaction that reduces an amount owed — issued to a customer to reverse or partially reverse an invoice, or received from a vendor to reverse a bill.
Fiscal Period
One of the accounting periods (typically monthly) making up a company's fiscal year. Journal entries are posted into a specific period, and the current period must be advanced as time moves forward.
General Ledger (GL)
The complete set of financial accounts and posted transactions that make up a company's books.
Journal
A category of transaction in FreeBooks, such as sales orders, purchase invoices, or POS sales. Every journal has a numeric journal ID (jID) used internally and in URLs.
Module
A self-contained functional area of FreeBooks (for example phreebooks, contacts, inventory, or phreepos), each with its own pages, menus, and permissions.
Passkey
A sign-in credential tied to a specific device (or synced between a user's own devices) that replaces typing a password, unlocked with a fingerprint, face scan, screen lock, or security key.
PhreeHelp
The context-sensitive help system built into FreeBooks, which this manual is part of.
Point of Sale (POS)
FreeBooks' in-person retail sales module, phreepos, used for ringing up sales at a till and taking payment.
Quote
A proposed sale (to a customer) or purchase (from a vendor) that has not yet been converted into an order.
Reconciliation
The process of matching a company's recorded bank transactions against a bank statement to confirm they agree.
Role
A named set of permissions assigned to users, controlling which pages and actions (identified by security IDs) they can access.
SKU
Stock Keeping Unit — the unique identifier for an inventory item.
Till
A cash drawer session in the Point of Sale module, opened at the start of a shift and closed/reconciled at the end.
Warehouse
A physical or logical location where inventory quantities are tracked separately.
